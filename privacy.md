# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 29 September 2026**

This policy explains how the **Anyone Can Sculpt Ice** internal assistant (the "application") handles information, including data obtained from Google services.

## What the application is

The application is a private, single-operator tool used by the owner of Anyone Can Sculpt Ice to run the business — bookkeeping, scheduling, correspondence and record-keeping. It is **not** a public product and has **no users other than the business owner** and the owner's appointed administrator.

## What it accesses

With the owner's explicit authorisation through Google's OAuth consent screen, the application may access the owner's **own** Google account data:

- **Gmail** — to read, search, draft and send business email as the owner
- **Google Calendar** — to create and read business appointments
- **Google Drive and Google Docs** — to store and edit business documents
- **Google Sheets** — to maintain business records such as schedules and task lists
- **Google Contacts** — to look up business contacts

## How the data is used

Data is used **solely** to operate Anyone Can Sculpt Ice: drafting replies, maintaining the business calendar, keeping bookkeeping and scheduling records, and preparing business documents. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the business.

## Storage and security

- Credentials and cached data are stored **locally** on the owner's own computer, protected by file-system permissions.
- Access is limited to the owner and the owner's appointed business administrator.

## Sharing

- Data is **never sold, rented or shared** with third parties.
- Nothing is transferred anywhere except to the Google services the application is authorised to use.
- The application's use of Google user data complies with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), which ends all access to Google data.
