Anyone Can Sculpt Ice

Ice Sculpting is accessible to everyone!

Get ready to unleash your inner artist at our ice sculpting workshop. A rare experience, only in Ottawa, the world's Ice Art Capital.

Our goal is to introduce participants to the fundamentals of ice sculpting small sculptures — from safety and tool handling to artistic technique — through a guided, indoor, hands-on experience.

About Me

With over 30 years of experience sculpting ice, I've competed in more than 50 international competitions around the world. My proudest achievement was earning first place at the World Ice Art Championships in 2009.

For a decade, I served as the lead organizer for Winterlude's ice carving events, coordinating large-scale public showcases. I've also contributed to the leadership of the Canadian Ice Carvers' Society, serving on the executive team in the past and remaining an active member today.

I've developed a one-of-a-kind workshop, available exclusively in Ottawa, where adults — and kids as young as 8 — can safely experience the thrill of ice sculpting through my Anyone Can Sculpt Ice introductory program.
