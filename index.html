Questions You Might Have

Is ice sculpting hard? I'm not artistic.

It's easier than it looks. Our guided process breaks it into simple steps. Most participants surprise themselves — seriously. You don't need artistic talent, just curiosity.

What should I wear?

Comfortable indoor clothes. We provide insulated gloves and aprons. The studio is climate-controlled — you won't be cold.

How long does the workshop last?

90 minutes from start to finished sculpture. Long enough to create something real, short enough to fit into your day.

Can I bring my child?

Absolutely — ages 8 and up are welcome. Under 12 needs a guardian present. It's an incredible parent-child experience.

When are workshops available?

Biweekly during Fall, Winter, and Spring. Workshops do not run in summer — the ice doesn't cooperate. Don't wait until the season ends.

What happens to my sculpture?

You take photos with it, admire it, and yes — it eventually melts. But the experience, skills, and pride last forever. (Some participants display theirs in a freezer at home!)

Built by a World Champion

Suguru Kanbayashi

World Ice Art Champion 2009 · 30+ Years · 50+ International Competitions

For three decades, Suguru has sculpted ice across the globe — from Fairbanks, Alaska to Harbin, China. He led Winterlude's ice carving events for 10 years and served on the executive team of the Canadian Ice Carvers' Society.

In 2009, Suguru and Junichi Nakamura took first place at the World Ice Art Championships with a 2.5-meter kingfisher sculpture carved from a single block.

He created Anyone Can Sculpt Ice to make ice sculpting accessible to everyone. Every workshop is led by instructors he personally trains, using the techniques he developed over a lifetime of competitive and professional carving.
